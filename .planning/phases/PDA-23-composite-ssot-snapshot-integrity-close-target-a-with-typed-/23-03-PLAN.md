- Secret/thinking/PII disclosure: resolver checks eligibility/privacy and defaults to metadata-only/sanitized output.
- Reference confusion: typed/prefixed refs are validated and unknown or ambiguous refs fail explicitly.
- Severity: high findings block completion.
</threat_model>
